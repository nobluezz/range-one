# Range One

A 5-versus-5 tactical shooter with heroes in your browser, plus a single-player campaign and online matches. Everything, the 3D engine included, is packed into one page.

**▶ Play: https://llHarrison.github.io/range-one/**

Works on Mac, Windows, Linux and Chromebook in any up-to-date browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Nothing to install.

Settings, personal bests and campaign progress are saved in your browser.
